Our story

My father started with one kadhai and a handcart

In 1998, my father, Shri Mohan Lal Sharma, set up a small kadhai outside the railway crossing on Station Road. He sold motichoor ladoo by the piece and samosas before school let out. I was fourteen and my job was washing the steel trays.

I still remember the smell of ghee at 4:30 in the morning and my mother pressing a hot jalebi into my hand before I went to class. When Papa's knees gave up in 2015, I took over the counter. The recipes, the same wooden ladle and the same early start did not change.

Today my wife and my younger cousin help in the kitchen. If you buy from us, you are buying from people who live two lanes away.

  • Pure desi ghee only, never vanaspati, in all mithai
  • Cooked fresh every morning, nothing reheated
  • Same family recipes since 1998
  • Clean kitchen, open for any customer to look at
  • Honest weight, we never pack the box with paper
